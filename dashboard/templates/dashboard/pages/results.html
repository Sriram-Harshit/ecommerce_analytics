📌 Strategic Operations Takeaway

Freight cost is the single strongest positive predictor of delayed fulfillment in the Brazilian e-commerce dataset. Higher freight costs typically represent long inter-state logistics routes (e.g. from São Paulo warehouses to Northern and North-Eastern regions) which encounter greater carrier transit variability.

🧠 Machine Learning Engineering Design

Due to severe class imbalance (late orders constitute ~6.8% of deliveries), standard accuracy is deceptive. This pipeline uses class_weight='balanced' and StandardScaler preprocessing in an automated Scikit-Learn Pipeline with in-memory caching to avoid retraining overhead on request cycles.
